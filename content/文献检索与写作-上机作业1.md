## 压裂工况下套管强度安全性分析及软件研发
### 论文详情：
英文题名： Casing Strength and Safety Analysis under Fracturing Condition and Software Development

中文姓名： 李嘉豪

姓名拼音： Li Jia Hao

学号： 22212040608

学院： 机械工程学院

学科： 0855机械

专业： 机械工程

研究方向： 页岩油水平井压裂过程中套损机理研究

导师姓名： 李明飞

导师单位： 西安石油大学机械工程学院

中文关键词： 弯曲载荷；交变载荷；水泥环缺失；套管强度安全性；软件开发

中文文摘：

水平井压裂技术提高了非常规油气资源的开采效率，但是，压裂施工过程中套管承受内外压差、轴向力、弯曲载荷以及交变载荷等复杂作用，会降低压裂套管的强度安全性，甚至引发套管变形或失效。因此，研究压裂工况下套管的受力特性及安全性评估方法，对于优化井身结构设计、保障油气井长期稳定运行具有实际工程意义。

本文采用理论分析与有限元仿真相结合的方法，系统研究水泥环缺失、内外压差、轴向载荷、造斜段弯曲载荷及交变载荷等复杂工况对套管强度安全性的影响，并基于研究成果开发一款套管强度安全性分析软件。研究内容包括以下几个方面：

（1）水泥环缺失对套管强度安全性影响分析。基于材料力学弯曲和弹性力学半逆解法等理论，建立压裂工况下套管的力学分析模型，推导轴向应力、径向应力、环向应力、切应力及等效应力分析公式。建立不同水泥环缺失形式的有限元模型，同时建立了不同狗腿度下15种常用规格套管的附加弯曲应力速查图表，方便工程技术人员迅速查找使用。分析发现，当水泥环缺失角度超过60°时，套管的局部应力显著增加，特别是在第二胶结面缺失情况下，套管的抗挤强度明显降低。此外，水泥环缺失导致套管受力状态不均匀，加剧了局部应力集中效应，易诱发塑性变形及疲劳损伤。

（2）造斜段弯曲载荷对套管强度的影响分析。建立造斜段套管-水泥环-地层三维有限元模型，在考虑压裂内外压差、温度和压裂引起的轴向载荷的基础上，用均布载荷简支梁模型表征狗腿度的影响，用弹性力学简支梁半逆解法，由狗腿度反推均布载荷，分析不同狗腿度（3°/30m、6°/30m、10°/30m）下的强度安全性。研究表明，随着狗腿度的增加，套管弯曲应力显著上升，弯矩效应导致一侧拉应力增大、另一侧压应力增强，并在局部形成高应力集中区域。当狗腿度超过6°/30m时，常用5寸半7.72mm壁厚套管，因弯曲引起的弯曲正应力达到51.20MPa。

（3）水泥环缺失+弯曲载荷+内外压差+轴向力+交变载荷共同作用下的套管强度安全性分析。建立造斜段套管-水泥环-地层三维有限元模型，在施加水泥环缺失+弯曲载荷+内外压差+轴向力的基础上，分析不同循环压力幅度及加载速率下套管的疲劳损伤特性和应力演变规律，考察交变载荷对弯曲段套管强度安全性的影响。研究发现，循环交变载荷作用对套管的强度安全性影响显著。对于本文的研究工况，超出套管的屈服强度概率较大，井筒完整性存在较大风险。

（4）压裂工况下套管强度安全性分析软件研发。基于上述研究成果，采用Visual Studio2022，并集成Access数据库，软件支持用户输入不同工况参数，基于数据管理、计算流程自动化及结果可视化等功能，开发压裂工况下套管强度安全性分析软件。软件可分析水泥环缺失、内外压差、轴向载荷、造斜段弯曲载荷及交变载荷等复杂工况对套管的强度安全性，并输出关键过程参数和变化曲线，实现了水平井压裂套管由井口到井底全井套管的强度安全性分析。

本文建立了一种压裂工况下套管强度安全性分析方法，揭示了水泥环缺失、弯曲载荷及交变载荷对套管安全性的影响机理，并开发了压裂工况下套管强度安全性软件，为油气田套管安全性评价、优化设计及施工参数调整提供了理论支撑。

外文关键词： Bending loads; Alternating loads; Missing cement rings; Casing strength safety; Software development

外文文摘：

Horizontal well fracturing technology improves the extraction efficiency of unconventional oil and gas resources, but the casing is subjected to complex effects such as internal and external pressure difference, axial force, bending load, and alternating load during fracturing construction, which will reduce the strength and safety of fracturing casing, and even lead to the deformation or failure of the casing. Therefore, it is of practical engineering significance to study the force characteristics and safety assessment methods of casing under fracturing conditions to optimize the design of well body structure and ensure the long-term stable operation of oil and gas wells.

In this paper, a combination of theoretical analysis and finite element simulation is used to systematically study the effects of complex working conditions, such as missing cement ring, internal and external pressure difference, axial load, bending load of diagonal section, and alternating load, on the strength and safety of casing, and to develop a casing strength and safety analysis software based on the results of the research. The research includes the following aspects:

(1) Analysis of the influence of missing cement ring on the strength and safety of casing. Based on the theories of bending of material mechanics and semi-inverse solution method of elastic mechanics, the mechanical analysis model of casing under fracturing condition is established, and the analytical formulas of axial stress, radial stress, annular stress, shear stress and equivalent force are deduced. The finite element models of different cement ring missing forms are established, and the additional bending stress charts of 15 kinds of commonly used casing specifications under different dog-leg degrees are also established, which are convenient for engineers and technicians to find and use quickly. It is found that when the angle of missing cement ring exceeds 60°, the local stress of the casing increases significantly, especially in the case of missing the second cement surface, and the crush strength of the casing decreases significantly. In addition, the missing cement ring leads to uneven force state of the casing, exacerbating the local stress concentration effect and easily inducing plastic deformation and fatigue damage.

(2) Analysis of the influence of bending load on casing strength in inclined section. A three-dimensional finite element model of casing-cement ring-formation is established in the inclined section. Based on the consideration of pressure difference between inside and outside of fracturing, temperature and axial load caused by fracturing, a simple beam model with uniform load is used to characterize the influence of dogleg degree, and a semi-inverse solution of elasticity-mechanics simple beam is used to inverse the uniform load from the degree of dogleg to analyze the safety of the strength in different degrees of dogleg (3°/30m, 6°/30m, 10°/30m). The study shows that with the increase of dogleg degree, the bending stress of the casing rises significantly, and the bending moment effect leads to the increase of tensile stress on one side, the enhancement of compressive stress on the other side, and the formation of a high stress concentration region in the local area. When the dogleg degree exceeds 6°/30m, the bending positive stress due to bending reaches 51.20MPa for the commonly used 5?-inch 7.72mm wall thickness casing.

(3) Strength safety analysis of casing under the joint action of missing cement ring + bending load + internal and external pressure difference + axial force + alternating load. A three-dimensional finite element model of casing-cement ring-strata in the inclined section is established, and on the basis of applying the missing cement ring, bending load, internal and external pressure difference, axial force, the fatigue damage characteristics and stress evolution of the casing are analyzed under different cyclic pressure amplitude and loading rate, and the influence of alternating load on the strength and safety of the casing in the inclined section is investigated. It is found that the effect of cyclic alternating load on the strength and safety of casing is significant. For the working condition studied in this paper, the probability of exceeding the yield strength of casing is larger, and the integrity of wellbore is at greater risk.

(4) Research and development of casing strength and safety analysis software under fracturing conditions. Based on the above research results, Visual Studio 2022 is adopted and integrated with Access database, which supports users to input different working condition parameters, and develops casing strength and safety analysis software under fracturing conditions based on the functions of data management, automation of calculation process and visualization of results. The software can analyze the strength and safety of casing under complex working conditions, such as missing cement ring, pressure difference between inside and outside, axial load, bending load of inclined section and alternating load, and output key process parameters and change curves, which realizes the strength and safety analysis of horizontal well fracturing casing from the wellhead to the bottom of the well.

This paper establishes a method for analyzing the strength and safety of casing under fracturing conditions, reveals the influence mechanism of missing cement ring, bending load and alternating load on the safety of casing, and develops a software for the strength and safety of casing under fracturing conditions, which provides theoretical support for the evaluation of the safety of casing, the optimization of the design, and the adjustment of the construction parameters in oil and gas fields.
